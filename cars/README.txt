Moses Benz Auto Care vehicle image library

All vehicle photographs use simple numbered slots in this flat folder:
  /cars/img1.jpg
  /cars/img2.jpeg
  /cars/img3.webp
  ...

The site checks .jpg, .jpeg, .png and .webp for each numbered slot and only renders a vehicle when its numbered image exists. Replace the slot files with the real car photographs you want published. The vehicle name, year and specifications come from js/cars.js — filenames do not need to contain model or year names.
