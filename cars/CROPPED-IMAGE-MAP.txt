MOSES BENZ — NUMBERED VEHICLE IMAGE SLOTS

The vehicle catalogue uses simple numbered image slots so real photographs can be replaced without changing code.

img1 -> first vehicle record in js/cars.js
img2 -> second vehicle record
img3 -> third vehicle record
...
img59 -> current final vehicle record

Supported extensions: .jpg, .jpeg, .png, .webp
The site tries those extensions automatically. Missing slots are not displayed as broken inventory cards.
