Mercedes-Benz Guide

Useful things to know about your Mercedes.

Short, practical guidance on maintenance, diagnostics, ownership and buying.

01

Servicing

Regular oil, filters, fluids, brakes, tyres and inspection help catch small problems before they become expensive repairs.

02

Diagnostics

A warning light is a symptom, not a diagnosis. Proper Mercedes-level scanning can identify the affected module and the fault behind it.

03

AIRMATIC

If the vehicle sits unevenly, raises slowly or drops overnight, suspension leaks, compressor performance and ride-height systems should be checked.

04

Buying a used Mercedes

Inspect the service history, scan the electronic systems, check suspension and drivetrain condition, and verify the vehicle carefully before purchase.

05

AMG

Performance Mercedes models deserve the same attention to service intervals, fluids, cooling, brakes and drivetrain condition as they give in performance.

06

When something feels wrong

Strange noises, rough starting, vibration, overheating, warning messages or unusual shifting are all useful information. Tell us what you noticed.
