Repair Stories

Before & After

One gallery per repair: Before → Process → After. Add as many process images as needed with the + button. Customers can swipe, use arrows or watch the gallery advance automatically.

Repair gallery

Trust & Credentials

Approvals & Certificates

Add only genuine workshop approvals, training certificates, memberships, registrations or other credentials. They will appear on the public Reviews page.

Remote Media

Cloudinary media

Use Cloudinary for the photos and videos. Your laptop can be off and the website will still load them. Configure the unsigned upload preset in js/cloudinary-config.js.

Upload images & videos

The upload button is ready for an unsigned Cloudinary upload preset. After upload, copy the returned URL into Inventory or Before & After.
